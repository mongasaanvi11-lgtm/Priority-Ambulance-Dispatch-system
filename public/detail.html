Triage Protocol WHO-IITT / AIIMS ATP / GVK EMRI Standards

How Patient Priority is Decided (RED, YELLOW, GREEN)

Calculated via standardized clinical evaluation: airway, breathing, circulation, disability, and complaint-specific danger signs (WHO-IITT / AIIMS / GVK EMRI).

RED PRIORITY < 8 min

Immediate Life Threat (Level 1)

Critical physiological collapse or severe trauma requiring immediate Advanced Life Support (ALS) with hospital ICU pre-alert (< 8 mins response).

Decision Criteria & Clinical Triggers:

Unconscious / unresponsive (AVPU), apnea or gasping, severe respiratory distress, airway obstruction or stridor, severe uncontrolled spurting bleeding, active continuous seizures, crushing chest pain radiating to arm with shock, acute stroke signs (FAST+), maternal trauma / head crowning / eclampsia fits.

Mandatory Fleet: Type-D ALS / Mobile ICU with Mechanical Ventilator
YELLOW PRIORITY < 20 min

Urgent Emergency (Level 2)

Serious condition requiring prompt paramedic intervention to prevent clinical deterioration (< 20 mins response).

Obstetric Protocol Rule: Per GVK EMRI 102 protocols, all pregnancy emergency calls are minimum YELLOW — never GREEN.
Decision Criteria & Clinical Triggers:

Audible wheezing, speaks single words, moderate dyspnea, excruciating abdominal pain without board rigidity, closed limb fractures, first-time seizure resolved, high persistent fever with severe dehydration.

Mandatory Fleet: Type-C Basic Life Support (BLS) with Oxygen & EMT
GREEN PRIORITY < 45 min

Non-Urgent / Stable (Level 3)

Clinically stable condition with normal physiological parameters (< 45 mins response).

Decision Criteria & Clinical Triggers:

Fully alert and responsive, normal comfortable breathing, clear airway, all stroke FAST signs strictly negative, mild abdominal pain, minor controlled cuts, isolated sprains.

Mandatory Fleet: Type-A/B Patient Transport Vehicle (PTV)

4-Step Priority Triage Workflow

1. ABCDE Survey

Checks unconsciousness, airway, and breathing distress. Any failure = Immediate RED.

2. Complaint Checks

FAST stroke tests, cardiac radiation, severe bleeding, burns extent evaluation.

3. Protocol Safeguards

Mandatory minimums enforced: Obstetric emergencies clamped to minimum YELLOW.

4. Fleet & Bed Match

Matches patient priority to ICU trauma capability and dispatches ALS/BLS fleet.

AIS-125 Specifications National Ambulance Fleet Categorisation

Ambulance Fleet Types: Meaning, Equipments & Deployment

Under AIS-125 standards and National Health Mission guidelines, ambulances are categorised by clinical equipment and medical crew qualifications.

TYPE A/B: PTV GREEN

Type A/B: Patient Transport Vehicle (PTV)

Non-emergency patient transfer between facilities with stretcher, first aid kit, and wheelchair ramp.

Onboard Equipment:

Patient Stretcher, First Aid Box, Wheelchair Ramp/Access, Splints.

Staffing / Medical Crew:

Trained Transport Driver & Patient Attendant.

Fleet Usage: 102 Routine Scheme & Private Networks
TYPE C: BLS YELLOW / GREEN

Type C: Basic Life Support (BLS)

Core emergency ambulance with Automated External Defibrillator (AED), oxygen cylinders, EMT crew, and spine board (108 Fleet).

Onboard Equipment:

Automated External Defibrillator (AED), Portable Oxygen Cylinders, Suction Unit, Spine Board, Cervical Collars, Vital Signs Monitor.

Staffing / Medical Crew:

Certified Emergency Medical Technician (EMT-Basic) & Pilot.

Primary Vehicle: Standard 108 Emergency Fleet
TYPE D: ALS RED Priority

Type D: Advanced Life Support (ALS)

Critical care road ambulance with mechanical transport ventilator, multipara cardiac monitor, intubation kit, and ACLS paramedic crew.

Onboard Equipment:

Multipara Cardiac Monitor / Defibrillator, Transport Mechanical Ventilator, Endotracheal Intubation Kit, Syringe Infusion Pump, ACLS Drugs & IV fluids.

Staffing / Medical Crew:

Advanced Paramedic & Emergency Nurse with ACLS certification.

Primary Vehicle: Critical 108 ALS / Trauma Corridor
MOBILE ICU Critical Red

Mobile ICU Ambulance

Tertiary inter-hospital transfer unit with ICU multi-channel ventilator, arterial pressure monitoring, and critical care intensivist physician.

Onboard Equipment:

ICU Multi-channel Ventilator, Arterial Blood Gas (ABG) Lab, Invasive Pressure Line Monitors, Dual Infusion Pumps, External Pacer.

Staffing / Medical Crew:

Critical Care Physician / Intensivist & ICU Specialist Nurse.

Fleet Usage: Tertiary Referral Hospitals & Private Networks
CARDIAC CARE STEMI / Heart Arrest

Dedicated Cardiac Care Unit

Specialized vehicle for heart attacks (STEMI) with 12-lead diagnostic ECG, direct catheterization lab telemetry relay, and cardiologist.

Onboard Equipment:

12-Lead Diagnostic ECG with Telemedicine Relay, Bi-phasic Defibrillator with Transcutaneous Pacing, Emergency Thrombolytic Stock.

Staffing / Medical Crew:

Cardiology Fellow / Specialist Cardiac Paramedic.

Fleet Usage: Heart Institutes & Super-Specialty Hospitals
NEONATAL / NICU Infant Life Threat

Neonatal / NICU Transport Unit

Specialized vehicle for preterm infants and pediatric emergencies with transport incubator, servo temperature control, and neonatologist.

Onboard Equipment:

Transport Incubator with Servo-Temperature Control, Neonatal Specialized Resuscitator, Micro-cuff Airway, Infant Pulse Oximeter.

Staffing / Medical Crew:

Neonatologist / Pediatric Critical Care Nurse.

Fleet Usage: Maternal & Child Health Referral Centers
Government (102 / 108) vs Private Fleet Regulation

Government fleets strictly provide PTV, BLS, and ALS free or subsidized. Specialized Mobile ICU, Cardiac, and Neonatal units operate under regulated tariffs at tertiary and private medical networks.
